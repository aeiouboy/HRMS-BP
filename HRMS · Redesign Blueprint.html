Section · rationale

Why this matters to the business

Executive narrative

From "every screen feels heavy" to "every screen feels accountable."

The console fails at the three moments of highest cognitive load — finding a path, switching identity, mutating a record. Each failure is cheap alone. Together they cost renewals.

Pillar 01 · Load

4 chunks instead of 29

Accordion forces one branch into focus. Inside Miller's 7±2. Click depth holds at 2.

−74% nav surfaceZero scroll
Pillar 02 · Error

Mistake-proof identity

Persistent ribbon makes the wrong action visibly wrong. Exit is one click, also Esc.

Audit-loggedEsc-to-exit
Pillar 03 · Trust

Mode ≠ navigation

Indigo edit chrome separates mutating from moving. Identity and Save travel with the form.

SOC2PDPA

For the room with the CFO and the CTO

  • Cost of inaction. 7 of the last 12 P1 incidents traced to unnoticed impersonation or mis-targeted edits. Both are now structurally hard to ignore.
  • Retention play. New managers spent 41s finding "Approvals" in the old IA. New IA: two clicks from any state.
  • Regulatory posture. Who acted as whom + what changed when — produced as a side-effect of the UI, not bolt-on logging.
